Gleamly

Clear duplicates and free up space on iPhone. Scanning happens on your device.

No account. Your photos never leave your device. Free to use with a daily allowance and ads. Gleamly Pro removes the limits and the ads.

Find the clutter

  • Similar photos, with the best shot kept for you
  • Similar videos and exact duplicates
  • Screenshots and blurred photos
  • Screen recordings, very short clips, unused burst shots and photos of documents
  • Browse everything sorted by size, biggest first

You decide what goes

Review groups or swipe to clean (left to delete, right to keep, with undo). iOS confirms every delete, and items go to Recently Deleted in Photos. Empty it there to get the space back.

More ways to save space

  • Compress: smaller copies of large photos and videos. Originals stay unless you choose to delete them.
  • Live to Still: keep the photo, drop the motion clip.

Secret Library

Keep chosen photos and videos behind a PIN, with Face ID or Touch ID if you turn it on. They're encrypted on this device and aren't backed up, so export anything you want to keep before deleting the app. Setting up a new Secret Library needs Gleamly Pro.

Widgets and charging animation

Battery and storage widgets (Gleamly Pro), and 6 full-screen charging animations. You can show one when you plug in by setting up a Shortcuts automation.

Free and Gleamly Pro

Free: scan and review everything, with a daily allowance for deleting and compressing, and ads. When you reach a limit you can watch a short ad to continue.
Gleamly Pro: no daily limits, no ads, the Secret Library and the widgets. An auto-renewing subscription (weekly or yearly) with a 3-day free trial on the weekly plan. Cancel any time in your Apple ID settings. See the Terms.

Privacy

Your photos and videos are never uploaded. Gleamly uses Google AdMob for ads and basic analytics to improve the app. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
