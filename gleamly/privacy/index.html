Gleamly Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026
App: Gleamly (iPhone and iPad)
Developer: Shruti Kapuriya

The short version

Your photos and videos stay on your device. Gleamly scans, groups, compresses and encrypts them on your device and never uploads them. We have no accounts and we don't ask for your name or email. The free version shows ads, and the app uses a few services from other companies for ads, subscriptions, notifications and basic usage statistics. This page says exactly what they receive.

Access to your photos and videos

Gleamly asks for access to your Photos library so it can:

  • scan your photos and videos on your device to find similar photos, similar videos, duplicates, screenshots, blurred photos, screen recordings, short clips, unused burst shots and photos of documents;
  • delete the items you choose;
  • save compressed copies and Live Photo stills that you ask for; and
  • move or copy the items you choose into the Secret Library, and export them back to Photos.

All of this happens on your device. Analysis uses Apple's on-device frameworks. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, never shared with ad or analytics services, and never leave your device.

If you allow access to selected photos only, Gleamly works with just those. You can change or remove access at any time in Settings > Gleamly > Photos. When you delete items, iOS asks you to confirm and moves them to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for up to 30 days unless you empty it.

What is stored on your device

  • scan results, such as file sizes and image fingerprints used to group similar photos, so the app doesn't have to rescan everything each time;
  • cleaning statistics, your settings (such as language and the charging animation style) and counters for daily free limits;
  • for the Home Screen widgets: your battery level, storage figures, the latest scan summary and whether Pro is active. These are shared between the app and its widgets on your device only.

Secret Library

The Secret Library lets you keep chosen photos and videos behind a 4-digit PIN, and Face ID or Touch ID if you turn it on.

  • Items in the Secret Library are encrypted on your device (AES-GCM) with a key that is created on your device and kept in your device's Keychain. The key is never sent anywhere and isn't included in iCloud Keychain.
  • Your PIN isn't stored. Only a salted hash of it is kept in the Keychain. Face ID and Touch ID are handled by iOS; Gleamly never receives your face or fingerprint data.
  • The Secret Library isn't synced to iCloud and isn't included in device backups.
  • If you delete Gleamly, or erase or replace your device, everything in the Secret Library is permanently lost and can't be recovered by us or by anyone. Export anything you want to keep to Photos first.
  • If you choose "Move", the originals are deleted from Photos and go to Recently Deleted. Empty Recently Deleted if you want them removed from Photos completely.

Data collected by services in the app

None of this includes your photos, videos, contacts, name or email address. We do not link it to your identity.

  • Advertising (Google AdMob). The free version shows ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law asks for it, Google's consent form is shown first. Ads use your advertising identifier only with your permission; without it they are less personalized. Gleamly Pro removes ads. Google's practices: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics). We receive which screens and features are used (for example that a clean finished, how many items and how many bytes, never which photos), your device model, iOS and app version, language, an app-instance identifier and crash logs, to fix bugs and improve the app. If you send feedback or a problem report from the app, the options you chose, the text you wrote (first 100 characters) and, for a report, your iPhone model, iOS and app version are sent this way.
  • Subscriptions (Apple and Adapty). Gleamly Pro is bought through Apple; we never see your payment details. Adapty receives your purchase and subscription status and an anonymous app user identifier to confirm that Pro is active and to restore purchases.
  • Notifications (OneSignal). If you allow notifications, OneSignal receives your device push token, language, app version and a few tags (for example whether Pro is active) to send you messages. You can turn them off in Settings > Notifications > Gleamly.
  • Ad measurement (Meta). Where you allow tracking and Google's consent allows ads, the Meta SDK may receive events such as finishing the intro, a first successful clean, viewing the paywall, and the value of an ad impression, with your advertising identifier, to measure our own ad campaigns.
  • Ratings. Gleamly may show Apple's rating prompt or a star sheet. A 1 to 3 star answer opens a short feedback form that is sent as described under usage statistics.

Tracking and your choices

  • When the app first asks to track you, tapping "Ask App Not to Track" stops access to your advertising identifier; ads stay but cannot use your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Gleamly.
  • In regions that require it, you can reopen Google's consent form in Gleamly's Settings > Privacy Options.
  • You can reset or limit ad tracking with your device settings, turn off notifications, remove photo access, or delete the app at any time.

Payments

Gleamly Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription. Payment is taken by Apple. Manage or cancel it in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. See the Terms of Use.

Data retention and your rights

Usage statistics and ad data are kept by the services above under their own policies and retention periods. You can ask us to access or delete data connected to your device (for example the app-instance identifier) by emailing us from the address below, and you can use the controls in the previous section. If you are in the EEA, UK or another region with data protection law, you also have the rights that law gives you, including to object and to complain to your data protection authority.

Children's privacy

Gleamly isn't directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region) and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above, and update the App Store privacy details before the app collects anything new.
