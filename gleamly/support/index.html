FAQ

I deleted photos but my storage didn't go up.

Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for up to 30 days. Open Photos > Albums > Recently Deleted and delete them there to free the space now.

Similar Photos says "Not available on this device".

Similar photo, video and screenshot grouping uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. Make sure Gleamly has photo access, and try again after the scan finishes.

Gleamly only sees some of my photos.

You allowed access to selected photos only. Go to Settings > Gleamly > Photos > Full Access.

How do I make the charging animation appear when I plug in?

In Gleamly, open Tools > Charging Animation and pick a style. Then open the Shortcuts app > Automation > + > Charger > Is Connected > Run Immediately, add the action Open URLs, and paste gleamly://charge. Tap the animation to close it. It also closes when you unplug.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit > Add Widget, search for Gleamly and pick a size. Widgets are part of Gleamly Pro; until you have Pro they show a lock.

I forgot my Secret Library PIN.

If you turned on Face ID or Touch ID, use it to unlock. Your PIN can't be recovered. On the lock screen, tap Forgot PIN? to erase the Secret Library and set a new PIN. This permanently deletes everything in it.

What happens to my Secret Library if I delete the app?

It's deleted permanently. It's encrypted on this device only and isn't in iCloud or backups. Export items to Photos first.

Does Gleamly cost anything?

Gleamly is free to use with a daily allowance for deleting and compressing, and ads. Gleamly Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription (weekly or yearly, with a 3-day free trial on the weekly plan) that removes the limits and the ads and unlocks the Secret Library and widgets. When you reach a free limit you can also watch a short ad to continue.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions, choose Gleamly Pro and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked.

In Gleamly open Settings > Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple ID you bought with. If it still doesn't unlock, email us the date of purchase.

Why do I see ads, and how do I change ad personalization?

The free version shows ads (Google AdMob). Gleamly Pro removes them. To change personalized ads, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Gleamly. In some regions you can also reopen Google's consent form in Gleamly's Settings > Privacy Options.
