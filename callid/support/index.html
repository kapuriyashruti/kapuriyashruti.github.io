Getting started

  1. Open Call ID and allow Contacts and Notifications when asked.
  2. Go to iPhone Settings > Apps > Phone > Call Blocking & Identification and switch on Call ID.
  3. Use the Search tab to look up any number.

Frequently asked questions

Blocked numbers still ring. What do I do?

Make sure Call ID is switched on in Settings > Apps > Phone > Call Blocking & Identification. If it is on, switch it off and on again, then open Call ID once so it can update the list.

Why does a number show the wrong name?

Names come from a licensed caller directory database, so they can be out of date. Save the number in your own contacts or in Call ID to see your own name for it.

How do I remove or correct the name shown for my number?

Email us at kapuriyashruti9@gmail.com with your phone number and we will remove or correct the name in our directory.

How do I report spam?

Search the number, tap Report spam and pick a reason. You can report each number once. Your report is anonymous: only the number and the reason are stored, and other users who look up that number then see it marked as spam.

I didn't get a notification after my call.

iOS only lets Call ID see a call end while the app is still running in the background. If iOS has closed the app, no notification appears. Open Call ID before or after important calls to keep it running.

Can Call ID record calls or show my call history?

No. iOS does not allow any App Store app to record phone calls or read the Phone app's history. Call ID logs calls you make from its keypad and calls that end while it is running.

How do I sync with my iPad or another iPhone?

Sign into the same Apple Account with iCloud on both devices. Call ID syncs through your private iCloud. Check the status in Call ID > Settings > iCloud.

Will cleaning up contacts delete anything without asking?

No. Merges, deletions and new photos only happen after you confirm each one.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove data from your device. To remove synced data, go to iPhone Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage. Call ID has no account to close.
