What you can do

  • Look up any number: see the name others saved it under, the country and spam reports.
  • Block spam calls: blocked numbers stay silent through iOS Call Blocking & Identification.
  • Report spam: flag fraud, telemarketing and robocalls to warn other users.
  • Smart dialer: country codes, lookup as you type and a camera number scanner.
  • Simple call CRM: lead stages, notes, tags and VIPs for your callers.
  • Follow-ups and scheduled calls: reminders that dial with one tap.
  • Contact cleanup: merge duplicates and fix empty or photo-less contacts.
  • iCloud sync: your data syncs across your own devices.

Private by design

No account, no ads and no tracking. Your contact book is never uploaded. Only the numbers you look up or report are sent to our lookup service.
