Privacy Policy — Call ID: Smart Number Lookup

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Call ID iOS app ("the App") uses, what leaves your device, and your choices. The App is published by Shruti Kapuriya ("we", "us").

Summary

  • No account or sign-up is needed, and we collect no personal data about you: no name, email, your own phone number, location or device identifier.
  • When you report spam, only the reported number and the reason you pick are stored on our server. Other users who look up that number then see it marked as spam.
  • No ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • Your address book is never uploaded.
  • Only phone numbers you look up or report as spam are sent to our lookup service.

1. Information sent to our lookup service

To show a caller's name and spam status, the App sends a phone number to our lookup server over an encrypted HTTPS connection when:

  • you search a number on the Search tab or type it on the keypad;
  • a call you logged is from a number that is not saved in your Call ID contacts; or
  • you report a number as spam (the number and the reason you picked, such as "Fraud" or "Telemarketing").

We do not send your name, your own phone number, your contacts, your location or any device identifier with these requests. The server returns the names listed for that number in a licensed caller directory database that we obtained from a third-party data provider, its country and its spam report count. Spam reports are counted anonymously and cannot be linked to you.

When you report a number, our server stores that number and the reason you picked. Other users who look up the number see it marked as spam, with the report count and the reasons. Nothing about you is stored with the report.

2. Information that stays on your device

  • Contacts: with your permission, the App reads your contacts to show caller names and to find duplicate, empty or photo-less contacts. Contacts change only when you confirm a merge, deletion or new photo. Contacts are never uploaded to us.
  • Camera: used only to scan a phone number from a card, poster or screen. Images are processed on the device and are not saved or uploaded.
  • Call notes and CRM data: contacts you save in Call ID, call notes, outcomes, follow-ups, scheduled calls, message templates and your block list are stored on your device.
  • Recent searches and the list of numbers you reported are kept on your device.

3. iCloud sync

If you are signed into iCloud, your Call ID data syncs between your own devices through your private Apple iCloud (CloudKit) database. We cannot see or access this data. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud.

4. Notifications

With your permission, the App shows local notifications after a call ends and for scheduled call reminders. These are created on the device.

5. Call blocking and caller identification

Numbers on your block list and caller labels are given to iOS through Apple's Call Directory extension. iOS uses this list on the device. The App cannot see, record or listen to your calls, and cannot read the Phone app's call history.

6. How long we keep data

Our lookup server keeps phone numbers and anonymous spam report counts to provide the lookup service. Search requests are not stored with any information that identifies you. Data on your device stays until you delete it or delete the App.

7. Sharing

We do not sell or rent any information. We do not share information with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can deny or remove Contacts, Camera and Notification access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • You can stop number lookups by not using the Search feature.
  • Deleting the App removes its data from your device. To remove iCloud data, go to iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.
  • If your own phone number appears in our caller directory and you want your name removed or corrected, email us from the contact below with the number and we will remove or correct it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us at the email below.
